Q3. What happens to the bounding boxes with update + fullRefit?
It ends up never switchings its bounding box from the original pair. So the bounding box constantly grows as they grow apart.

Q4. What happens with reset (rebuild every frame) instead?
It rebuilds the bounding boxes and actually reconstructs the bounding box to better fit dynamically which one is the closest.

Q5. Why are some circles drawn with their sides cut off?
When spawned, it only allows pixels to be drawn in the window. Some circles that spawn close to the edges, with large enough radiuses have 
their pixels cut off due to the edge of the screen.
